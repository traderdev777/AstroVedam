GATE 2 — RESOLVE THE APPARENT-POSITION AND ABERRATION CLAIM

2.1 The Mathematical Decomposition of the Pipeline

The production calculation in src/engines/astrology/ephemeris.js lines 749–767 computes:

$$\vec{\Delta}_{\text{prod}} = \vec{r}_S(T_{\text{tau}}) - \vec{r}_E(T), \quad \text{where } T_{\text{tau}} = T - \frac{\tau}{36525}, \; \tau = \frac{d_0}{c}$$

$$\lambda_{\text{prod}} = \operatorname{atan2}(\Delta_y, \Delta_x) + \Delta\psi$$

Let us rigorously separate each physical effect:

1. Instantaneous Geometric Position ($t$): $$\vec{D}_{\text{geom}}(t) = \vec{r}_S(t) - \vec{r}_E(t)$$ 2. Light-Time Retardation (Target Motion): Light left Saturn at time $t - \tau$. During light transit $\tau = D / c$, Saturn moved by $\Delta \vec{r}_S \approx -\tau \vec{v}_S(t)$. Therefore, the vector from Earth at $t$ to Saturn at $t - \tau$ in the Solar System Barycenter (SSB) inertial frame is: $$\vec{\rho} = \vec{r}_S(t - \tau) - \vec{r}_E(t) \approx \vec{D}_{\text{geom}}(t) - \tau \vec{v}_S(t)$$ This is the Astrometric Direction. 3. Observer-Velocity Stellar Aberration: The observer on Earth moves through the SSB inertial frame at velocity $\vec{v}_E(t) \approx 29.8\text{ km/s}$. In special relativity (and classical aberration to order $v/c$), a telescope moving at velocity $\vec{v}_E$ must be pointed forward along the direction of motion to intercept incoming photons: $$\Delta \hat{u}_{\text{stellar}} = +\frac{\vec{v}_E}{c} = +\frac{\tau \vec{v}_E}{D}$$ Therefore, the true apparent direction vector in the Earth observer's moving rest frame is: $$\vec{u}_{\text{apparent}} \propto \hat{\rho} + \frac{\vec{v}_E}{c} = \frac{[\vec{r}_S(t - \tau) - \vec{r}_E(t)] + \tau \vec{v}_E(t)}{|\vec{\rho}|} \approx \frac{\vec{D}_{\text{geom}}(t) - \tau (\vec{v}_S - \vec{v}_E)}{|\vec{\rho}|}$$ 4. The Audit Challenge: Notice what the production code actually evaluated: $$\vec{\Delta}_{\text{code}} = \vec{r}_S(t - \tau) - \vec{r}_E(t)$$ The production code evaluated $\vec{\rho}$, NOT $\vec{\rho} + \tau \vec{v}_E$! It computed the light-time corrected astrometric position, but omitted the observer velocity aberration vector $+\vec{v}_E / c$!
